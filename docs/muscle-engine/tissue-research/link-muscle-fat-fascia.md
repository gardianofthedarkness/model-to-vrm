# Link: muscle compartment → deep fascia → fat

Desk research, 2026-11-24. Every number gives its source. *Unverified* means: seen only in a search snippet or secondary citation, or my own derivation, and not checked against the primary text. No datasets were downloaded.

Layer order, from the inside out (Stecco group terminology):

```
muscle fibres ─ epimysium (epimysial fascia) ─ HA-rich loose layer ─ DEEP FASCIA (2–3 collagen sheets + loose interlayers)
  ─ deep adipose tissue (DAT, oblique retinacula cutis profundus) ─ SUPERFICIAL (membranous) FASCIA
  ─ superficial adipose tissue (SAT, vertical retinacula cutis superficialis) ─ dermis
```

## 2. Deep fascia mechanics

**Tensile (fascia lata, Bonaldi 2023, 4 fresh-frozen donors aged 54–89, 0.5 %/s to failure):**

| Direction | Young's modulus (linear region) | UTS | strain at break |
|---|---|---|---|
| longitudinal, stiff sheet (L1) | 55.7–191.9 MPa (donor means) | 2.1–12.0 MPa | 12–16 % |
| transverse / second sheet | 4.5–18.9 MPa (one outlier 119.9) | 0.5–9 MPa | 8–23 % |
| ratio L/T | 4.5–28 × | | |

Two-family Holzapfel–Gasser–Ogden fit (elastic region ≤ 3–4 % strain), per donor: C₁ 0.11–0.68 MPa, k₁₁ 2.8–5.7 MPa, k₂₁ 42–173, k₁₂ 0.77–2.0 MPa, k₂₂ 115–579, κ 0.0004–0.19 (Table 3).
Viscoelasticity: residual stress ≈ 56 % at 60 s and ≈ 43 % at 300 s, two-term Prony series (Table 2). Crural fascia loses 40 % stress in ≈ 120 s (Stecco 2014).

**In vivo:** shear-wave elastography of fascia lata gives a composite value through skin, fat and muscle (Germain & Gibaud 2026, [arXiv:2606.03353](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03353), ramp-relaxation in vivo, fibre-recruitment model; numbers not in abstract). ITB shear modulus in kPa exists in Kyoto-group SWE papers ([repository.kulib.kyoto-u.ac.jp](https://repository.kulib.kyoto-u.ac.jp/dspace/bitstream/2433/198147/1/j.gaitpost.2014.12.006.pdf)) — values not read.

**Pre-tension:** no quantitative measurement found. Pre-stress is asserted conceptually (Stecco/Levin, encyclopedia entry) — *unverified*, open question.

**How fascia limits bulging (compartment pressure):**
- Vastus medialis intramuscular pressure rises **linearly with force up to MVC**, peak recorded **570 mmHg**; slope depends on depth from the fascia; fits a Laplace-law model of curved fibres (Sejersted et al. 1984, [doi:10.1152/jappl.1984.56.2.287](https://doi.org/10.1152/jappl.1984.56.2.287), catheter, 7 men).
- Resting IMP 14 (2–31) mmHg in knee extensors (1986 study, [PubMed 3788624](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3788624/)); rest ≈ 4.7 mmHg in leg (snippet, *unverified*).
- Clinical thresholds for an over-tight compartment: pre-exercise ≥ 15, 1 min post ≥ 30, 5 min post ≥ 20 mmHg ([JSSM 2008](https://www.jssm.org/jssm-07-96.xml-Fulltext)).
- For the engine: pressure inside the fascial shell during contraction is the observable. 570 mmHg ≈ 76 kPa at MVC is the upper bound.

## 4. Under pressure (sitting, external load)

- **Linder-Ganz et al. 2007** ([doi:10.1016/j.jbiomech.2006.06.020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbiomech.2006.06.020)), open MRI, 6 subjects, FE: gluteus peak compressive strain 74 ± 7 %, 32 ± 9 kPa; fat 46 ± 7 %, 18 ± 4 kPa; IT interface pressure 17 ± 4 kPa measured. Interface law in the FE: not in abstract.
- **Al-Dirini et al. 2016** (Ann Biomed Eng, [Adelaide record](https://digital.library.adelaide.edu.au/items/89172083-441f-4edd-9e71-d0cee4d65cab)): FE validated to 4.7 ± 4.4 mm vs weight-bearing MRI. **Al-Dirini et al. 2017** Clin Biomech 50:70 (10 men): muscle keeps deforming through both load steps, **fat deforms little after the first step**.
- **Zappalà et al. 2024** ([doi:10.1016/j.jbiomech.2023.111913](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbiomech.2023.111913)), MR digital volume correlation on the Al-Dirini data, 10 subjects: **gluteus maximus slid away from the ischial tuberosity by 13.74 mm on average** (bursa allows it); peak shear strain in the **fat next to the muscle interface**; registration error 0.99/1.78 mm.
- **Sonenblum et al. 2015** (RESNA, [html](https://www.resna.org/sites/default/files/conference/2015/wheelchair_seating/sonenblum.html)), upright MRI, 7 subjects: tissue under the IT is mostly **fat and connective tissue**; 5/7 had no meaningful muscle under the IT; muscle moved away from the IT on foam; fat+skin under IT 13 ± 9 mm.
- **Oomens et al. 2003** CMBBE 6:171 ([TU/e](https://research.tue.nl/nl/publications/can-loaded-interface-characteristics-influence-strain-distributio/)): high shear strain in muscle near bone and in the fat layer; support-surface parameters change fat shear much more than muscle shear.
- Buttock FE models in this line often **tie** bone/muscle, muscle/fat, fat/skin (Macron et al., snippet via [INSA course PDF](https://moodle.insa-lyon.fr/pluginfile.php/275766/mod_folder/content/0/Sitting-HiFi.pdf)), and the authors flag tying as a limitation.

**Reading:** under seat load the large slip is **muscle over bone/bursa** (≈ 14 mm), not fat over fascia. Fat compresses and shears near the muscle boundary; Wilke's 0.7 vs 5.7 mm shows the fat+superficial fascia is coupled to the deep fascia far more than to the muscle. Direct MRI evidence of fat sliding *over* deep fascia under load: **not found**.

## Recommendation for the engine

**Fascia model.** Deep fascia (fascia lata, crural fascia, septa) as a **thin anisotropic membrane shell**, not tets:
- thickness from the atlas region: 0.7–1.0 mm crural (Pirri 2021), ≈ 0.7–0.95 mm fascia lata (Bonaldi 2023 histology; US values ~1.6–1.9 mm include epimysium) — scale with the elf only by a rule stated in E-MAT (open question below);
- material: two-family HGO per Bonaldi 2023 Table 3 (use the donor mean and spread as a prior for the judge loop), fibre family 1 along the limb axis, family 2 at the measured 75–80°;
- viscoelastic: two-term Prony, ≈ 56 % residual at 60 s (Bonaldi Table 2); matters for T8 and the long hold in T5;
- E_L ≈ 50–190 MPa on a sub-mm shell is near-inextensible next to muscle (kPa). In Newton VBD treat it as a stiff membrane; if the step cannot carry it, Romeo-style XPBD distance constraints are the cited fall-back.

**Interface laws.**

| Interface | Law | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| muscle epimysium ↔ deep fascia | **sliding, no separation** (FEBio `sliding-elastic`, `tension=true`) with low friction; μ **not cited → learn it** in the judge loop against T5/T6 data | HA layer (Stecco 2011); shear strain 56–121 % across ~2 mm (Langevin 2011, Wang 2023); muscle 5.7 mm vs superficial fascia 0.7 mm (Wilke 2020) |
| muscle ↔ muscle inside compartment | sliding, no separation | same plane type |
| septa ↔ femur (linea aspera), fascia ↔ bony prominences | **tied** | superficial fascia adheres at prominences (Stecco reappraisal) |
| deep fascia ↔ fat (DAT) | **tied through a compliant layer** (keep DAT as its own soft tets, or a cohesive shear spring) — not frictional sliding | Wilke 2020 coupling; DAT oblique retinacula (Stecco); no MRI evidence of fat sliding over fascia under load |
| muscle ↔ bone at IT (bursa) | sliding | gluteus slides 13.7 mm off the IT (Zappalà 2024) |
| fascia compartment pressure | observable, not a parameter | IMP linear with force, ≤ 570 mmHg at MVC (Sejersted 1984) |

This also changes the plan's line "fat slides a little over fascia": the cited data say the fat+superficial fascia **follows** the deep fascia (≈ 0.7 mm) while the **muscle slides under the fascia** (≈ 6 mm) and **off the bone** (≈ 14 mm).

**Which test scene each dataset checks.**

| Test | Data |
|---|---|
| T1 pack integrity | fascia thickness per region: Pirri 2021, Bonaldi 2023, Stecco 2011 |
| T2/T3 contraction | compartment pressure vs force: Sejersted 1984 (linear, depth-dependent) |
| T4 activation stiffness | SWE datasets (Mendeley, Zenodo VL/MG); in vivo fascia lata (Germain & Gibaud 2026) |

| T8 settle | fascia relaxation (Bonaldi, Stecco 2014) — no in vivo jiggle data for fascia found |

## Open questions

1. Friction coefficient / shear stiffness of the epimysium–fascia HA plane: no measurement found (rabbit TLF tribology paper is the lead).
2. Fascial pre-tension: no in vivo value; needed instead of a "shrinkage" knob.
3. Iliotibial band thickness and stiffness in vivo (kPa values in Kyoto SWE papers not read).
4. Does fat slide over deep fascia under seat load? No direct measurement; Zappalà's DVC fields could answer it — check whether they resolve the fascia plane.
5. How to scale thickness and stiffness to a 0.98 m stylised body (not addressed by any source).
6. Bonaldi data are 4 elderly donors, elastic fit only to 3–4 % strain; Stecco 2014 is 1 donor at 120 %/s.
7. Primary texts still to check: Han 2024 and Romeo 2020 coupling details; Linder-Ganz/Al-Dirini interface laws; Zappalà dataset licence.
